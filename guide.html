01 / GET STARTED

Open your studio

Option A — Use it online

  1. Click Open Studio ↗.
  2. The public beta starts with a generated demo model and your starter card effects.
  3. Try a preset, adjust the orbit, or load your own saved project.

Option B — Run it locally on Windows

  1. Download the beta ZIP and extract it fully.
  2. Install Node.js LTS if needed.
  3. Double-click START-STUDIO.cmd and keep its console window open.
  4. Open http://127.0.0.1:8787/content.html in Chrome or Edge.
Do not open content.html as a file:// page. Some media, storage, and export functions require HTTPS or localhost. VS Code Live Server can also serve the folder.
02 / SOURCE

Choose your live source

In the Scene tab, choose one of the supported video sources: generated test video, VDO.Ninja stream, your webcam, or no model.

  1. For a remote avatar, select VDO.Ninja model.
  2. Enter your own VDO.Ninja stream ID or view link.
  3. Click Connect source once the sender is live.
  4. Enable chroma key if the video has a green, blue, or custom-color background.

If you choose My webcam, browser permission is requested only when you click Connect source. Audio is not received or recorded by the editor.

03 / CREATE

Build your scene

Use Scene for model size, keying, and orbit controls. The common orbit can be ring, helix, or wave; the movement can be full rotation or a left-to-right swing.

Use Cards to customize each of up to 36 slots individually. Add PNG/JPG/WebP/GIF media; adjust visibility, size, glow, distance, rotation, and position. Add stuff supports further overlay layers, and Effects includes presets, backgrounds, and particles.

The maximum of 36 applies to card slots. Other layer and media upload limits are listed in the included README. More objects and animated sources can increase CPU/GPU usage.
04 / SAVE

Keep your work

Your browser can autosave your project locally. But browser storage can be cleared or differ between OBS and your regular browser, so export a backup.

  1. Open the OBS / Save tab.
  2. Click Download project to save a portable .json backup.
  3. Use Load project to restore a saved project later.
  4. Keep related media safe; linked online sources require those links to stay available.
05 / OBS

Bring your creation to OBS

  1. Start the editor locally using START-STUDIO.cmd.
  2. Prepare your scene and click Download clean OBS scene inside OBS / Save.
  3. Save the exported scene-output.html in the same folder as content.html and keep its css, js, cards, and vendor folders beside it.
  4. In OBS, add a Browser Source pointing to http://127.0.0.1:8787/scene-output.html.
  5. Set a width of 1920, height of 1080, and 30 or 60 FPS according to your setup. Refresh OBS after re-exporting changes.
OBS Virtual Camera is started from OBS, not the website. When using OrbitFX with a call app, choose OBS Virtual Camera in that app. Do not feed OBS Virtual Camera back into the same scene as its own input.

For more details, read the original OBS guide ↗.

06 / SHORTCUTS

The fast controls

HHide or show the editor UI
SpacePause/resume scene motion
BTrigger confetti

Keyboard shortcuts do not fire when you are typing inside an input field. The Full Screen button uses the browser fullscreen API. GIF, movie and live feeds can continue moving when you pause the studio's own animations.

07 / TROUBLESHOOTING

Something not working?

My model is missing

Make sure your VDO.Ninja sender is live, your ID is correct, and click Connect source again. The generated demo video should work without a remote stream.

My webcam will not connect

Use HTTPS or localhost, allow access when prompted, and avoid video feedback loops. Certain embedded browsers restrict webcam access.

My OBS scene looks different

OBS and your normal browser don't share the same autosave. Export a fresh clean OBS scene and place it beside the rest of the editor files.

The animation is slow

Reduce card count, glow, particles, animated GIFs, video sources, resolution or OBS FPS. Small download size does not imply low graphics processing cost.

08 / PRIVACY

Your beta, your projects

The public beta contains no personal live VDO.Ninja stream ID. You must connect your own stream manually. Uploaded media is processed in the browser unless you choose to export and publish it. The bundled VDO.Ninja SDK contacts its providers when you connect remote video.

Project JSON and exported clean scenes may contain your uploaded images, settings, and stream identifiers. Treat them as personal files and review them before sharing publicly. This is a beta: some browsers, devices, and third-party embeds may behave differently.
